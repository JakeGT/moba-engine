# Brand and key art: brief for marketing and the graphic designer

**Status:** v0 brief, 2026-10-31. **Leads:** marketing executive and graphic designer. This is
a short brief: agents recorded the constraints and the material that already exists. The
brand decisions are theirs.

## Fixed points

- **The game's name and logo are brand marks:** they stay **all-rights-reserved** and are
  listed as such. They are the only art in the repo that isn't CC0 or CC-BY (roadmap §9.6).
- **Fidelity:** full photoreal (Cyberpunk-2077-class fidelity, not a cyberpunk setting).
- **Setting:** science fantasy; tone **epic, weathered**; two factions, **the Solar Forge** and
  **the Void** (working names: see [world-and-factions.md](world-and-factions.md)).
- **Camera investment:** the engine plans zoomed views, spectator cinematics and a hero
  **showcase** (champion select, victory) with showcase LODs, strand hair, path-traced skin
  and depth of field (roadmap §3). Key art and trailers can be rendered **in engine** with
  that preset.
- **AI-generated material:** concepts may be AI-assisted (local open-weight models, CC0 with
  provenance; roadmap §9.5). Steam's AI disclosure is generated from the provenance
  manifests, so marketing gets the disclosure list for free.

## What the brand needs to decide

1. **The game's name** (and the public faction names, if the working ones change).
2. **Logo and wordmark.**
3. **The key-art idea.** Agents' draft directions, mocked up in the review app:
   - *The Crossing*: sunlit Forge brass in the west against floating Void stone in the east,
     across the basin at golden hour. The two factions in one frame.
   - *Light vs gravity*: a Kiln Warden's halo blazing against a Still Oracle's lensing dark,
     a tight two-hero composition.
   - *The fallen engine*: the Builder rib arching over the Crossing, both armies tiny below
     it. Scale and mystery.
4. **Store capsules** (Steam's header, small, main and vertical capsules) built from the key
   art; a legible logo at the small capsule size (231 × 87) is the hardest constraint.
5. **Trailer look:** in-engine showcase preset vs pre-rendered.
6. **Showcase and victory framing:** camera moves, lighting rig and background per faction.

## Constraints from the rest of the bible

- Marketing art can be freer than gameplay (no readability clamps), but **heroes must look
  like their in-game models**: build key art from the showcase LOD, not from un-shippable
  concept-only designs.
- Team colours (green/blue/red) are a gameplay convention, not the brand palette. The brand
  palette should come from the faction palettes ([palette-and-value.md](palette-and-value.md) §5):
  Forge brass and white-gold against Void black, pewter and violet-white.
