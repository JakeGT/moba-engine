## The evidence scene: a scripted 5v5 teamfight replay

`tests/replays/teamfight-5v5.mbr` (103,628 bytes, 1,800 ticks, 6 checkpoints) is recorded by
`cargo run --release -p moba-tools --bin teamfight`: ten scripted heroes (no bots, no minion
waves, default content, seed `0x7EA3F16E00050005`) march to a rally point north of mid lane,
out of tower range, and fight — Q/W/E in rotation at the nearest enemy hero whenever the
cooldown allows, basic attacks in between, respawn and walk back. Recording twice gives
byte-identical files, `moba-tools replay` verifies it (`state hash 0x8945433b3adbd4ce`,
`verified true`), and `crates/sim/tests/teamfight_replay.rs` fails if a simulation change ever
stops it verifying (regenerate it then). The fight starts at tick 319 and contains:

| Event | Count |
|---|---:|
| ability casts (all 15 built-in abilities; 2–10 each) | 96 |
| projectiles / projectile hits | 36 / 30 |
| basic attacks | 269 |
| hero deaths / respawns | 10 / 8 |

The client plays it with `MOBA_REPLAY` in the local view (the camera follows the live heroes'
centroid at `MOBA_CAMERA_DISTANCE`, default 46 m, as in network play), firing every effect of
`content/vfx/` from the simulation's events.

The "1440" and "2160" rows asked for 2560×1440 and 3840×2160 windows; this display caps the
window at **2560×1380** (the `extent` column), so neither the High 1440p nor the Ultra 4K
target resolution was measured. They are Ultra/High settings at 2560×1380.

**Reading:**
- **Every tier is far inside its §7 GPU budget.** At 1080p the whole lit, shadowed frame is
  0.081–0.114 ms of GPU work at p50 on Low–High (0.29–0.32 ms on Ultra) and at most 0.343 ms
  at p95; at 2560×1380 it is 0.52–0.77 ms at p50 (p95 at most 0.82 ms), against budgets of
  6–14 ms. The VFX passes add 0.011 ms at p50 on Low (no resolve), 0.022 ms on Medium/High
  and 0.026 ms at `x8` (1080p); 0.08–0.17 ms at 2560×1380 and on Ultra. The frame interval is
  display-paced at 4.177–4.179 ms (240 Hz) in every run, with or without VFX. CPU time inside
  the render call is 0.076–0.086 ms at p50 with VFX and 0.066 ms without (the spawn upload
  and the uniform block; the emitters run before the render call).
- **The Ultra and 2560×1380 rows ran with the GPU in a slower clock state.** Their `scene`
  pass, which does not depend on VFX, takes 0.10–0.11 ms (Ultra, 1080p) and 0.33–0.44 ms
  (2560×1380) at p50 against 0.049–0.064 ms on Low–High at 1080p, more than the pixel and
  shadow-filter ratios explain, and `vfx_simulate` moves the same way (0.041 ms on Ultra at
  1080p against 0.008 ms on High, for a buffer four times larger); the RTX barely leaves idle
  clocks at this load and the later runs of the session stayed lower. Compare passes within a
  row, not across rows run in different clock states.
- **What costs what.** `vfx_simulate` (three dispatches over the whole buffer plus a 128-byte
  copy) is 0.008 ms at p50 at 1080p on Low–High, the same at `x8`: it scales with the
  buffer capacity, not the live count, because every slot is visited. `vfx_draw` follows the
  live count and the fill: 0.003 ms on Low and 0.008 ms on Medium/High at the shipped
  emission, 0.006–0.012 ms at `x8`. `vfx_resolve` is a fullscreen pass on Medium and up
  whether or not an alpha particle is live: 0.006 ms at 1080p, 0.009–0.027 ms at 2560×1380.
  None of the VFX passes depends on the shadow pass or the lighting set: the frame graph
  orders `vfx_simulate` before `shadow`, and `vfx_draw` after `scene`.
- **Fill rate.** The overdraw estimate's p95 is **0.09–0.10** screens at the shipped
  emission on every tier (max 0.25), **0.58–0.63** at `x8` (max 1.67) and **1.16** at `x16`
  (max 3.17). Every run stays under its tier's overdraw budget (Low 1.5, Medium 3, High 5,
  Ultra 8) even at its worst instant: Low peaks at 1.05 at `x8`, and the `x16` run (Ultra) at
  3.17.
- **Particle counts.** The shipped effects keep 36 particles alive at p50 and about 205 at
  p95 (max about 690) over the fight; `x8` reaches about 2,850 and `x16` 5,330 — 11 % of
  Ultra's 49,152 and more than Low's 4,096 (Low was run at `x8` only, max 1,961). The budgets
  therefore leave a large margin for richer art. The budget logic itself (importance order,
  the telegraph and enemy-projectile exemptions, focus-based culling, the fallback cubes) is
  covered by unit tests in `crates/client/src/app/vfx/tests.rs`; the shipped scene does not
  reach it.
- **GPU timing noise.** At a few hundredths of a millisecond the p95 columns mostly measure
  clock ramps; the pre-R2a run of the same matrix (unlit scene, no shadow pass) had the VFX
  passes within 0.001 ms of these at p50 on every Low–High 1080p row.
- **Not measured here:** the other §7 reference GPUs (UHD 620, GTX 1060, RTX 3060 classes and
  Apple M1) are still unowned (#9/#127), so "at budget on every tier" is shown on one GPU with
  each tier's settings, not on each tier's reference hardware.

## Validation and synchronisation validation

`MOBA_VULKAN_VALIDATION=required` (the Khronos layer with synchronization validation
enabled by the renderer), every tier's default lighting (shadow pass on), animated 3D
portraits and a minimap re-bake every 30 frames (`MOBA_PORTRAITS=animated
MOBA_MINIMAP_BAKE=30`), and `MOBA_VFX_SCALE=8`. The teamfight replay keeps every VFX pass,
both blend paths, soft particles and mesh particles (Shield Bash crystals, the Cleaving Throw
cube) busy; the R7b offscreen passes run only in network mode, so a `MOBA_SERVER=local` match
(bots, skinned heroes) covers the frame with every pass at once:

| Device | Run | Low | Medium | High | Ultra |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| lavapipe | replay capture at tick 460 (`capture_frame` fails on any validation error) | 0 messages | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| RTX 5070 Ti | replay, 1,500 frames from tick 330, vsync off | 0 messages (max 1,910 live) | 0 (2,762) | 0 (2,849) | 0 (2,845) |
| lavapipe | network, 600 frames | 0 messages | 0 | 0 (132 live) | 0 |
| RTX 5070 Ti | network, 3,000 frames: shadow + portraits (50–103 runs) + minimap (97 runs) + VFX | 0 messages (212 live) | 0 (214) | 0 (212) | 0 (214) |

The lavapipe network runs reach the first casts only on High (the software device renders the
early match slowly), so lavapipe's particle coverage comes from the replay rows.

The effect previews (`MOBA_VFX_PREVIEW`, every sample effect including the mesh ones) and the
golden captures below were also validation-clean on lavapipe. The graph's new access kinds
have headless barrier tests (`crates/render/src/graph/tests.rs`,
`crates/render/src/frame_graph.rs`): compute write → vertex storage read and → indirect read,
depth write → read-only depth + fragment sampling (`DEPTH_STENCIL_READ_ONLY_OPTIMAL`), the
persistent cross-frame import, the OIT targets and the stats copy's final host read.

## Goldens

Both `render-golden.yml` captures, run exactly as the workflow does (lavapipe, validation
and sync validation required, release build, the default tier High so every VFX pass is in
the frame), on `main` @ `33a67ad` (R2a's regenerated goldens) plus this branch, match their
fingerprints byte for byte (`mean_abs_diff=0.000 max_diff=0`): no
effect is live at the local golden's frame 90 or the networked golden's pinned tick 120, and
the VFX passes leave an HDR target with no particles unchanged. The goldens were not
rebaselined.
